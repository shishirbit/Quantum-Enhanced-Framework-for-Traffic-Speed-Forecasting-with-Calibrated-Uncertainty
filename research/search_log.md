## Core query families

1. `(quantum OR variational quantum OR QNN OR QGNN) AND (traffic forecast* OR traffic flow prediction)`
2. `(quantum annealing OR QAOA OR QUBO) AND (transportation OR routing OR traffic signal)`
3. `(METR-LA OR PEMS-BAY) AND (graph neural network OR transformer) AND traffic forecasting`
4. `(quantum machine learning) AND (benchmark* OR classical baseline OR entanglement)`
5. `(variational quantum circuit) AND (data re-uploading OR barren plateau OR noise)`

## Inclusion criteria

- Direct transportation/traffic quantum application, or a method paper needed
  to justify the quantum design and fair comparison.
- Peer-reviewed source preferred; original preprint retained only when
  publication status was not verified and is explicitly labelled.
- Public metadata sufficient to verify title, authors, year and venue/DOI.
- Classical traffic papers selected for protocol influence, recent strength,
  or an official reproducible implementation.

## Exclusion criteria

- Network packet traffic unless it supplied only general QML methodology.
- "Quantum-inspired" heuristics treated as physical gate-model evidence.
- Secondary blogs for bibliographic facts.
- Unverifiable performance tables or copied cross-paper numbers with different
  splits, masks or horizons.
- Predatory, retracted, or bibliographically incomplete sources.
